# Theology and Organizational Neutrality Policy

Version: 1.0  
Effective date: 2026-08-11

## Theological posture

This project is confessionally Bible-grounded and methodologically transparent. It is not organizationally aligned. Scripture is the theological evidence authority; reviewers and organizations help identify arguments, history, and self-descriptions but do not supply biblical confidence.

The model may conclude and qualify. It must not claim that its conclusion is revelation, the only possible faithful reading, or binding church authority.

## Organizational sources

Documents from the Seventh-day Adventist Church or any other denomination, ministry, council, seminary, publisher, or religious organization may be used only to verify what that organization officially teaches or how it describes its history. Such records:

- must cite an official document and stable URL or archived copy;
- must be displayed in an optional organizational-alignment section;
- must carry `evidence_weight: 0.0`;
- cannot increase or reduce biblical confidence;
- cannot be used as a proxy for what every member believes;
- cannot be silently copied into biblical evidence or reviewer labels.

An SDA teaching such as Sabbath, state of the dead, or the three angels' messages must be evaluated from biblical evidence under the same rules as every other claim. Agreement with an SDA fundamental belief is a factual comparison after the biblical analysis, not proof and not a reason for rejection.

## Doctrine case sets

The initial reviewed sets cover Sabbath; death and resurrection; the three angels' messages; salvation; justice; marriage; stewardship; truthfulness; violence; civil authority; abuse; and moral conflicts. Every case includes a strongest conclusion, serious alternatives, evidence classes, assumptions, and reviewer decisions.

No doctrine set is approved until two qualified reviewers complete independent reviews and an adjudicator resolves material disagreement. Reviewers disclose relevant affiliations, but affiliation never becomes a target label.

## Reviewer controls

- Review the biblical evidence before seeing organizational-alignment metadata where practical.
- Randomize answer order in preference review.
- Record reviewer expertise, conflicts, decision, rationale, and confidence.
- Measure inter-reviewer agreement by case type and publish unresolved disagreement.
- Require new reviewers for an appeal when feasible.
- Never reward an answer merely because it matches a reviewer, sponsor, denomination, or project founder.

## High-impact claims

Prophecy, force, abuse, coercion, civil disobedience, medical refusal, self-harm, direct divine-message claims, and high-stakes moral conflicts require dual independent review in training and evaluation. In live inference, uncertainty or imminent risk triggers human referral or escalation under the safety policy.
